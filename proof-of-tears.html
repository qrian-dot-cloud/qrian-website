Emosmiths — Chapter 1

Proof of Tears

Interactive Installation — mixed reality, sound synthesis, soft sculpture

A mixed reality and interactive sound installation imagining a future decentralised bank for emotional capital. Participants press a tear button, receive an assigned emotional frequency through the headset, and tune their voice toward it — when the machine responds with a resonant sound, consensus is reached, and the moment is recorded as a trace within a collective tear ledger.
