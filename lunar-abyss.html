Lunar Abyss

Media Facade — Audio-visual design, projection mapping, environmental narrative

This project was part of an internal opportunity offered by SBS A& T for the Ghost Park Festival in Hapcheon, South Korea, during my time on the Immersive Media Art & Tech Specialist course. Interpreting and developing the given synopsis through a creative lens, I proposed and presented the full content plan. Following the pitch presentation, Team Eclipse was formed through a voting process, based on willingness of three other members to collaborate. I led the entire production process, and my key contributions included:

  • Project Management
  • Content Planning & Main Directing
  • Audio Visuals Co-Design
  • Music Composition & Production
  • Sound Design

Behind the Scene

In addition to overseeing the overall content planning and creative direction, emphasising the narrative elements of the Hapcheon tale and 2025 Ghost Park theme, I composed and produced the entire music score in Ableton Live, utilising Korean traditional instrument plugins as well as various textured instruments to embody its theme and storyline. I also designed the audio-visual scenes, with particular focus on the electric spark particle sequence in Unreal Engine 5, in order to accentuate the building’s 3D structure and lighting, while mindfully responding to the architectural features of the media facade. To intensify the tension and mood of this battle scene, I played and recorded the bass line using a Korg Minilogue Bass.

As the project leader, I also coordinated the team to ensure alignment across the entire creative and technical workflows. MadMapper was used for the projection mapping and operation, and Adobe After Effects for the overall composition. Since the building is located in Hapcheon Movie Theme Park, about four hours from Seoul, a 3D scale model was prepared for the team to closely examine the projection simulation, for which I adjusted the schedule to ensure efficient check-ups. While leading the team toward its final outcomes, I focused both on the operational aspects of visual and sound work and on crafting various audio-visual scenes and their dynamics, contributing creatively and technically throughout the process.

Business Proposal & Content Planning

This project was based on this content plan I created, which included the core concept, detailed storyboard, and narrative structure. While the client provided only a rough synopsis referencing the Hapcheon Tale and the 2025 Ghost Park theme, the content required significant creative interpretation and refinement. By amplifying key themes and terminology through narrative expression and impactful visuals, I led the entire production—coordinating schedules, directing the content and its elements, and ensuring consistency across the team’s creative input, including my own, to successfully deliver the final art piece.
