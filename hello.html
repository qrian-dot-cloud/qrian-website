Kyuri Kim, born in Seoul, South Korea, based in London, United Kingdom, is a media artist and musician creating under the name QRIAN.

With a background in singer-songwriting, electronic music production, and live performance, QRIAN's practice explores socio-political discourse, cross-cultural identity, emotion and state, and human-machine reconfiguration.

She released her debut EP QRIAN in 2019, with all tracks self-produced and self-performed. By creating the event BYULGORAE, she has supported and hosted gigs for Seoul's local electronic musicians, collaborating with them through live electronic music and voice performance. Rooted in a deep interest in language, voice, and performance, she has expanded her practice to incorporate audiovisual elements and field recording, and recently, interactive and embodied computational systems - such as gesture, machine listening, and mixed reality - have become her mediums.

QRIAN currently examines how resonance between humans and machines can reshape our perceptions of authenticity, creating vivid encounters through multi-sensory stimuli and narratives. Her recent project, Emosmiths: Proof of Tears, draws on decentralised systems and speculative forms of sci-fi capital fiction to question how human emotion might be valued and eventually integrated into increasingly mechanised social systems.

Her academic background spans MA in Computational Arts, BMus in Popular Music, having studied at Goldsmiths, University of London, and FC in Popular Music and Sound Technology from the Liverpool Institute for Performing Arts (LIPA). QRIAN has been developing her practice-based research, using her own artistic and technical process as a form of inquiry.
